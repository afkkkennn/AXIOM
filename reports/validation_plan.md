# Validation and promotion gates
- Preserve alpha.5 pilot and alpha.6 predeclared holdout; do not tune on the evaluated 2025–26 season and describe it as untouched.
- Compare models by Brier, log loss, calibration, accuracy and MAE on identical games. A small point estimate difference is not evidence of robust superiority. For future confirmation, use chronological folds and uncertainty intervals with date-clustered bootstrap.
- Historical market source without quote timestamps is retrospective only. Strict comparison requires contemporaneous paired quotes, provenance and tipoff timestamps. Do not claim Bet88 coverage without verified Bet88 quotes.
- Injury model: require point-in-time reports and player impact estimates that were available then, plus baseline expected rotations and replacement players. A postgame DNP label is not pregame availability.
- Player model: score availability separately from appearance-conditional forecasts; record projected and actual minutes, rate errors, and joint PRA uncertainty.
- Live ledger: record forecasts before tipoff with timestamps and source data; retain all misses, settle once finals are verified. Empty ledger means no live evidence. No retrospective backfill represented as live.
- An experimental version is not promoted until a genuinely subsequent holdout supports it.
